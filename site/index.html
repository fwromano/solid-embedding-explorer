What this picture shows · data and projection methods

All-row PCA is newly fitted to combined real rows. Original five-solid balanced PCA and semantic fits stay immutable; new shapes projected out of cohort for those fits. PLS is refitted on all 94,200 views across ten shapes, with equal total weight for every shape/paint pair. It is an exploratory fit with no held-out accuracy claim. Semantic shape/color axes are class-mean SVD fitted only on balanced TRAIN headings 0° and 20°. These supervised displays are not held-out accuracy, text similarity, or calibrated confidence. Prefixes are renormalized before fitting and exact cosine comparison.

All dots are actual catalog rows. Ideal continuous heading aliases are metadata, not extra points or exact finite-mesh pixel equivalences. A sphere has a full rotational alias: heading and elevation are not independently observable, even though actual elevation images are retained. Binary projection coordinates follow catalog order; the source row ID is not an array offset. Paths connect measured rows only within the same shape, paint, and elevation; dashed links bridge gaps and the canonical period seam. Display filters never refit axes. An explicit build refresh may change PCA-all.

White outline marks the inspected row; a dashed crosshair identifies an occluded point. The image and highlighted point share one row ID. Source images have their original neutral background; no synthetic previews or fallback points are generated.

PLS is weighted multi-target regression on shape and paint one-hot blocks and/or observable pose contrasts. Each shape/paint pair receives 1/60 of the total weight, and each included target block has equal weighted variance. Heading uses sin/cos of the symmetry-aware canonical phase where observable; elevation uses sin/cos where observable. Unobservable coordinates receive zero centered contrast: cylinder, cone and torus supply no heading signal, and sphere supplies no pose signal. All 94,200 existing embeddings contribute; these latent variables are exploratory display axes, not held-out accuracy or PCA percentages. Plot colors still use physical heading, not canonical pose phase.
